Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SWP withdrawals, step-ups, inflation protection, and capital longevity.

What is the difference between SWP and a regular mutual fund dividend option? ▼
Dividends (IDCW) are declared at the discretion of the fund house and depend on distributable surplus, making payouts unpredictable and fully taxable at your income tax slab. In contrast, an SWP allows you to choose a predictable, fixed monthly payout, and only the capital gains portion is taxed, making SWP substantially more tax-efficient.
Why should I step up my SWP withdrawals every year? ▼
Living expenses and healthcare costs increase each year due to inflation. Withdrawing a flat ₹50,000 per month today will buy significantly less 10 years later. Stepping up withdrawals by 5-6% annually ensures your lifestyle and purchasing power remain fully protected throughout retirement.
How does SWP offer better tax efficiency than bank Fixed Deposit interest? ▼
In a bank Fixed Deposit, 100% of the interest earned each year is taxed according to your income tax bracket, and TDS is deducted automatically. In an SWP, each withdrawal is treated as a redemption of capital and gains; only the profit component is subject to capital gains tax (with equity LTCG exempt up to ₹1.25 Lakhs per financial year), leaving a much higher post-tax income in your hands.
What happens if market returns are lower than my withdrawal rate? ▼
If the rate of withdrawal exceeds the portfolio's annualized return, the underlying capital begins to erode. FinNomy's SWP calculator includes an automated Longevity Depletion Check that alerts you to the exact timeline (years and months) until capital exhaustion, allowing you to optimize withdrawal amounts in advance.
Can I stop, pause, or change my SWP withdrawal amount at any time? ▼
Yes. Mutual fund SWPs offer complete liquidity and flexibility. You can alter the withdrawal amount, change payout frequency, pause payouts, or redeem the remaining lump sum balance at any time without locking penalties.
